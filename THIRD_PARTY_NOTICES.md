## Unclutter

Declutter (`Sources/Webkit95Kit/Declutter.swift`, `Sources/webkit95/DeclutterScript.swift`,
`Sources/webkit95/Declutter.swift`) ports ideas and short pieces of code from Unclutter by Kitze,
https://github.com/kitze/unclutter, at commit 9ef9beccc1e57b4e3115ae68644b8fc9c19c29f6: the candidate
extraction and clutter pattern, the stable selector grammar, the cookie notice test, the page kind
and route family rules for template keys, the reversible hiding with an attribute, an owned
stylesheet and inline display overrides, the Jev question instructions and criteria, and the
redaction patterns.
