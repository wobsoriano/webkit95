Weekend outlook: rain clears by Sunday

A slow moving front will bring steady rain to the valley on Saturday, with totals between ten and twenty millimeters. The heaviest rain should fall in the afternoon, and there is a small chance of thunder in the hills to the north.

Skies clear from the west overnight, and Sunday looks dry and bright with highs near eighteen degrees. Winds will be light. Monday starts cool, with fog possible in the low lying areas near the river, before another sunny afternoon.

Farmers should expect the fields to be workable again by Tuesday. The longer range models point to a settled week, with no significant rain until the following weekend.
