Weekend promo: two tickets for the price of one on the harbor ferry

Buy any return ticket on Saturday or Sunday and bring a friend for free. The promo covers every crossing before six in the evening.

Promo for members: a free coffee with the morning paper

Show the member card at any harbor kiosk before nine and the coffee is on us. The promo runs every weekday this month.

Ferry timetable changes for the autumn

The evening crossings move fifteen minutes later from the first of October, so the last ferry leaves the city pier at a quarter to eleven. The morning timetable does not change. Tickets bought before the change stay valid, and the season pass covers the new departures without any adjustment.
