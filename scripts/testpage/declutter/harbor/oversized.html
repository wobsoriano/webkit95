Harbor bridge repairs to close one lane through November

By the Harbor Times staff

Repairs to the harbor bridge deck will close the inbound left lane from next Monday until the end of November, the transport office said on Tuesday. Crews will replace expansion joints and resurface two hundred meters of the deck on the city side. The work runs overnight where possible, but the lane stays closed by day because the fresh surface needs time to cure before it can carry traffic.

The office expects delays of ten to fifteen minutes at the morning peak and asks drivers to use the tunnel or the ferry where they can. Buses keep their timetable, and the cycle path on the north side stays open throughout the works. A second phase, covering the outbound lanes, is planned for the spring and will be announced separately once the contractor confirms the schedule.

Residents of the harbor district raised the noise of overnight work at last week's council meeting. The contractor has agreed to stop cutting and grinding after eleven at night and to keep generators on the far side of the bridge. A hotline for complaints opens on Monday morning.
