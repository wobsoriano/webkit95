Library extends weekend hours

By Dana Whitfield, City Hall reporter

Share this story: TwitterFacebookLinkedInEmail

The public library will stay open until eight in the evening on Saturdays and Sundays starting next month, the library board announced on Monday. The change follows a year of requests from students and working parents who said the current weekend hours were too short.

The board said the longer hours are paid for by a donation from a local foundation and by moving two part time positions from weekday mornings, when the reading rooms are quietest. article-marker-5521 Librarians will also add a homework help desk on Sunday afternoons.

Board chair Samuel Ortiz said circulation rose by twelve percent last year, the highest growth in a decade, and that the study rooms are booked solid every weekend. The library will review the new hours after six months.

The branch libraries on the north side will keep their current hours for now. The board said it will look at extending them if the foundation renews its gift next year.

Residents can reserve study rooms online or at the front desk. The library also plans to extend Wi-Fi to the courtyard so that people can work outside when the weather is good.
