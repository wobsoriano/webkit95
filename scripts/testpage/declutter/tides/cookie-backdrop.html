High tide moves to the early evening this week

High water at the harbor mouth comes at ten past six on Monday and about fifty minutes later each day after that, reaching a quarter to ten by Friday. The range is small all week, under three meters, so the low water walk to the island stays open for two hours either side of low tide.

Boats leaving the inner harbor should plan for the sill, which clears only for three hours around high water. The harbor office posts the exact times each morning on the board by the fuel dock and on the recorded line.
